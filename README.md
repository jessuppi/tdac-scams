This repository documents suspected scams, misleading websites, and deceptive commercial services targeting travelers applying for the Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC).

The TDAC is a mandatory online arrival form introduced by Thailand's Immigration Bureau on May 1, 2025, replacing the traditional paper TM.6 arrival and departure card. All foreign travelers entering Thailand by air, land, or sea are generally required to complete the TDAC online no more than three days (72 hours) before arrival, subject to limited exemptions.
