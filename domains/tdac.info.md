# tdac.info

## Government Impersonation

The domain `tdac.info` closely resembles the name of Thailand's official Digital Arrival Card service and prominently presents a TDAC application form through its privately operated website. Its branding and application process may lead travelers to mistake it for an official government portal. Although the website states that it is not affiliated with the Thai government, its prominent TDAC branding may still create confusion about the service's identity and authority.

## Misleading Fees

The website charges USD $29 per traveler to submit Thailand's Digital Arrival Card (TDAC), even though the official government application is completely free. Its homepage prominently promotes a fast, simplified application process and encourages travelers to begin entering personal information before clearly presenting the fee farther down the page. Although the website eventually acknowledges the free government alternative, its presentation may lead travelers to believe that payment is necessary or provides special processing advantages. Thailand's Ministry of Tourism and Sports has explicitly warned travelers against third-party websites requesting payment for TDAC registration.

## Deceptive Promotion

The website prominently claims to be trusted by 10,987 travelers and displays a 4.9 rating alongside customer testimonials, without clearly identifying an independent platform where these figures can be verified. It also promotes rapid approval, guaranteed delivery, and professional error checking while repeatedly emphasizing the risks and difficulties of using Thailand's free government application. These promotional tactics may exaggerate the service's credibility and advantages, encouraging travelers to pay USD $29 for assistance that is not required to submit the official TDAC.
