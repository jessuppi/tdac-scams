# tdac.info

Last updated: October 28, 2026

## Government Impersonation

The domain `tdac.info` and its homepage title, "TDAC Thailand 2026: Your Digital Arrival Card in 5 minutes," prominently use the official form's name. The homepage encourages visitors to begin a TDAC application before disclosing its USD $29 fee. Although a brief "Independent filing service · Not the government portal" notice appears above the application form, the fuller non-affiliation disclaimer is placed at the bottom of the page. This presentation may mislead travelers arriving from search results into believing they are using Thailand's official TDAC service at `tdac.immigration.go.th`, which is free, rather than a paid third-party intermediary charging USD $29 per traveler.

## Misleading Fees

The website charges USD $29 per traveler to submit Thailand's Digital Arrival Card (TDAC), even though the official government application is completely free. Its homepage prominently promotes a fast, simplified application process and encourages travelers to begin entering personal information before clearly presenting the fee farther down the page. Although the website eventually acknowledges the free government alternative, its presentation may lead travelers to believe that payment is necessary or provides special processing advantages. Thailand's Ministry of Tourism and Sports has explicitly warned travelers against third-party websites requesting payment for TDAC registration.

## Deceptive Promotion

The homepage claims it has filed TDAC forms more than a thousand times without providing independent verification. It also claims the official government portal takes most travelers 30–60 minutes to complete, despite Thailand's Immigration Bureau estimating approximately 3–5 minutes per person when the required information is ready. The website's FAQ falsely claims the government portal offers "no support," although official assistance is available by email and through immigration officers. It further claims passport and date-format errors cause most TDAC rejections and promotes its manual checks as protection against these problems, without providing supporting evidence. These misleading comparisons exaggerate the difficulties of using the free government portal to promote its USD $29-per-traveler service.

## Hidden Disclosures

The homepage invites travelers to begin a TDAC application before displaying its USD $29-per-traveler fee. The website accepts applications up to 12 months before arrival and says they are held until the official 72-hour filing window. However, another homepage section promises submission "within thirty minutes during Bangkok hours," while its Terms and Conditions state that applications are submitted "shortly after payment confirmation." These inconsistent descriptions fail to clearly distinguish between accepting payment, preparing an application, and actually submitting it to Thai Immigration. Travelers may therefore be misled about when their applications are filed and what processing speed their payment guarantees.

## Misleading Claims

The website's FAQ claims the official TDAC portal has "strict photo upload rules that often fail on mobile," although the government's passport-image scanning feature is optional and travelers can enter their details manually. It also claims families must submit separate applications, despite the official portal supporting up to 10 travelers per group, and says submitted cards cannot be edited, although an "Update Arrival Card" function exists. Separately, the website lists diplomatic and government passport holders as exempt, although non-Thai diplomatic passport holders must submit TDAC. These inaccurate comparisons misrepresent government requirements and capabilities while encouraging travelers to purchase its USD $29 assistance service.

## Privacy Concerns

TDAC.info’s application form says personal information is “never stored on our servers after processing.” Its privacy policy says the service collects passport, contact, and travel data, keeps unpaid drafts and internal order records, and retains filed applications while they remain relevant to a trip and as long as transaction records are required. The policy gives no fixed maximum retention period. These disclosures conflict about post-processing storage and leave the retention period unclear.

## Billing or Service Issues

TDAC.info promises a full refund when its own error causes a TDAC rejection or when it fails to file before departure. Its separate Refund Policy repeats these guarantees and permits cancellation any time before filing. However, its Terms and Conditions initially restrict refunds to duplicate payments or requests made within 30 minutes before processing begins, while a later section allows cancellation until submission. More seriously, both documents state that refunds are unavailable once a TDAC has been submitted to Thai Immigration, despite also promising refunds when the website's own errors cause a rejection after submission. These contradictory policies leave customers uncertain whether the advertised refund guarantees will actually be honored.
