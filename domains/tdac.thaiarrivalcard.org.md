# tdac.thaiarrivalcard.org

Last updated: October 31, 2026

> [!NOTE]
> As of October 31, 2026, the website redirects to a Lovable Trust & Safety takedown notice. The findings below describe previously indexed website content.

## Government Impersonation

The `tdac.thaiarrivalcard.org` subdomain and homepage prominently used Thailand's official Digital Arrival Card name, emphasized that the form was mandatory, and encouraged travelers to apply through its private service. The website also promised delivery of an "approved QR code" by email, potentially suggesting official immigration processing. Although its homepage footer and About page disclosed that the service was privately operated, unaffiliated with the Thai government, and optional, these explanations were separate from its primary application messaging. This presentation could mislead travelers arriving from search results into mistaking the website for Thailand's official TDAC application portal.

## Misleading Fees

The website described its paid TDAC assistance fee as being "in addition to any official government fees that may apply," despite Thailand's Immigration Bureau providing TDAC registration completely free of charge. This wording could confuse travelers about whether additional government charges existed. More seriously, an October 25, 2026 Trustpilot reviewer alleged that the website advertised a USD $10 service fee but charged their card USD $269.97 after payment details were entered. Although this reported charge has not been independently verified, the substantial alleged discrepancy raises serious concerns about the transparency of the website's pricing.
