# tdac-thailand.com

Last updated: November 8, 2026

## Hidden Disclosures

The website offers paid assistance with Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) applications and identifies itself as a private service. However, its published Terms and Conditions leave the operating company's name, tax identification number, and registered office address blank in the section intended to identify the business. The same terms provide `info@tdac-thailand.com` as a website contact but identify `info@malaysia-mdac.com` as the merchant-of-record contact for payments and refunds, without naming that merchant or explaining its relationship to the TDAC service. Although the website discloses that assistance carries a service charge, these omissions make it difficult for prospective customers to identify the contracting business and the entity responsible for collecting payments.
