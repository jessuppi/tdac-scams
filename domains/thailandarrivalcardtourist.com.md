# thailandarrivalcardtourist.com

Last updated: November 1, 2026

> [!NOTE]
> As of November 1, 2026, `thailandarrivalcardtourist.com` no longer displays its TDAC application service. The domain currently shows a generic page indicating that it may be available for sale. The findings below document the website's previous activities and historical customer complaints.

## Misleading Fees

On April 8, 2026, U-Tapao International Airport published an Immigration Bureau warning specifically identifying `thailandarrivalcardtourist.com` among unofficial websites charging travelers for TDAC registration, which the Thai government provides free of charge. Separately, a traveler reported paying USD $50 through the website and being offered an additional USD $20 for express delivery within four hours. Although this individual account has not been independently verified, it raises concerns about charging substantial fees for a free government registration service.

## Hidden Disclosures

Although the website reportedly disclosed that it was not affiliated with the Thai government, its application process raised concerns about when travelers learned they were using a paid third-party service. One customer reported completing the TDAC application fields before encountering a USD $50 payment request. The customer had reached the website through a sponsored Google search result and initially failed to recognize it as an unofficial service. This experience suggests that important distinctions between the free government registration and the website's optional paid assistance were not sufficiently apparent to some visitors before they began providing their personal and travel information.

## Misleading Claims

A historical website scan recorded the site's description promoting its service as a way to obtain a "Thailand Visa On Arrival" online. This wording misleadingly associated its paid TDAC application service with obtaining a Thai visa, despite the Thailand Digital Arrival Card being an arrival registration form rather than a visa or authorization to enter the country. Thailand's Immigration Bureau explicitly confirms that the TDAC does not replace a visa. Such language could confuse travelers about what they were purchasing and whether the service provided any immigration benefit beyond submitting the mandatory arrival form.

## Billing or Service Issues

A traveler reported that the website advertised a refund guarantee, encouraging dissatisfied customers to request their money back. After discovering that official TDAC registration was free, the traveler said they submitted a refund request through the website's contact form but received no response. The complaint has not been independently verified, and it remains unclear whether the operator eventually responded or issued a refund. Nevertheless, the reported experience raises concerns about the reliability of the website's advertised refund policy and customer support.
