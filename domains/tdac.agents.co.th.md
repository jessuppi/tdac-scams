# tdac.agents.co.th

Last updated: October 28, 2026

> [!NOTE]
> As of October 28, 2026, tdac.agents.co.th directly hosts a privately operated TDAC submission website, with visitors from tdac.in.th redirected here.

## Government Impersonation

The `tdac.agents.co.th` homepage prominently displays the official name of Thailand's Digital Arrival Card service and an application button. The page advertises "TDAC Cost: FREE" and "Instant Approval," potentially giving travelers the impression that they are using an official government filing portal. A disclaimer at the top explicitly denies affiliation with the Thai government and directs visitors to `tdac.immigration.go.th`. Nevertheless, the official-sounding branding and application prompts may still cause confusion about the service's privately operated status, particularly among travelers redirected from `tdac.in.th`.

## Misleading Fees

The website prominently advertises "TDAC Cost: FREE" and "Instant Approval" alongside its application button, yet charges USD $8 for submissions made more than 72 hours before arrival. These paid early submissions are merely held until Thailand's official 72-hour submission window opens, meaning travelers receive no earlier government processing or approval. This arrangement may mislead travelers into believing that paying provides earlier access to the TDAC system or a necessary registration benefit. Thailand's Immigration Bureau provides TDAC registration completely free of charge, and the Ministry of Tourism and Sports has explicitly warned travelers against third-party websites requesting payment for TDAC registration.

## Deceptive Promotion

The service advertises free TDAC submissions and instant approval while displaying live traveler activity and more than 1,400 comments. Its associated "TDAC Scam Alert" page labels 29 other websites as scams or untrusted, including describing `tdac.info` as a "CONFIRMED SCAM." Meanwhile, the same page lists its own fee-charging service alongside Thailand's official government portal under "Trusted TDAC Submission Options." This self-promotional comparison may create the misleading impression of an independent authority evaluating TDAC websites, encouraging travelers to trust its private submission service over competing providers without independently substantiating its rankings.

## Hidden Disclosures

The TDAC submission page displays a government non-affiliation disclaimer near the top and provides a link to the official immigration portal. However, its main application area prominently advertises "TDAC Cost: FREE" and "Instant Approval" alongside the application button. The USD $8 charge for submissions more than 72 hours before arrival appears in a separate comparison table, while the explanation that early applications remain pending until the official registration window opens appears farther down the page. Separating these details from the primary application prompt may cause travelers to overlook the conditions of the free service and misunderstand what the paid option provides.

## Misleading Claims

The service advertises "Instant Approval" alongside its application button and lists an approval time of 0–5 minutes. However, TDAC submission produces an arrival-card confirmation, not immigration approval or permission to enter Thailand. Under "How Does the TDAC System Work?", the website also claims group submissions support up to 100 travelers, while its comparison table lists the limit as "Unlimited." Thailand's Immigration Bureau permits only 10 travelers per official group submission. Although the higher figures may describe the intermediary's handling of multiple applications, this distinction is not clearly explained. These claims may mislead travelers about the official TDAC process and its capabilities.

## Privacy Concerns

The private TDAC application collects sensitive passport, contact, and travel information. Its operator's privacy policy permits sharing personal information with affiliates, service providers, and sister companies, including for marketing purposes. However, it also states that personally identifiable information will not be shared with third parties, without clearly explaining how this restriction applies to those recipients. A separate data protection policy likewise permits sister-company sharing for promotional purposes. Although the privacy policy offers a self-service option for deleting personal data, neither policy specifies how long TDAC submission records are retained when no deletion is requested. Travelers therefore lack clear information about the default retention period for their passport and travel records.

## Billing or Service Issues

Public complaints about this TDAC submission service describe payment demands, unexpected charges, refund requests, and unanswered support inquiries. A reviewer on the `tdac.in.th` Trustpilot page reported encountering a payment demand at the final application stage and abandoning the process without paying. Separately, a commenter on `tdac.agents.co.th` reported being charged within the advertised free 72-hour window, including duplicate charges involving two applicants. Another comment requested a refund after payment. A reviewer on the `tdac.agents.co.th` Trustpilot page reported difficulty completing an application because of an address-code issue, with live chat and email inquiries going unanswered for more than six weeks.
