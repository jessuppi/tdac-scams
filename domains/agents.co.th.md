# agents.co.th

Last updated: October 28, 2026

> [!NOTE]
> The TDAC submission service promoted on agents.co.th is also available through tdac.agents.co.th, which receives visitors redirected from tdac.in.th.

## Misleading Fees

The company advertises free TDAC submissions within 72 hours of arrival but lists "Arrival >72h" at USD $8 (270 THB) under "How We Improve Upon the Original System." This presents early submission as a capability unavailable through Thailand's official government portal. However, the associated private service explains that applications submitted early remain pending until the official registration window opens. Paying therefore allows travelers or tour operators to provide information in advance, not obtain earlier government processing or approval. Without that distinction alongside the pricing comparison, travelers may misunderstand the benefit provided by the additional fee.

## Deceptive Promotion

The company describes its TDAC Agents Portal as revolutionizing group submissions and advertises a "10x" efficiency gain without explaining how that figure was measured. It also promotes support for 76 languages versus five on the official government portal, alongside unlimited travelers per submission. Although the company describes practical features for tour operators, including grouped downloads and individual application edits, its promotional figures are not accompanied by benchmark data or independent verification on the page. These comparisons may exaggerate the service's demonstrated advantages and encourage travelers or tour operators to favor its private portal over Thailand's free government application.

## Hidden Disclosures

The company identifies its offering as a "Third party TDAC portal" and explains that submissions within 72 hours of arrival are free. However, its comparison table advertises "Arrival >72h" for USD $8 (270 THB) against "N/A" for the official government system without explaining that early applications are held until the official submission window opens. That processing limitation appears on its separate TDAC website rather than alongside the comparison. Although the company discloses its commercial status and pricing, separating these details may lead travelers to believe that paying provides earlier government processing rather than advance collection and scheduling of their information.

## Misleading Claims

The company's TDAC comparison table lists "Resubmission" as "Supported vs Not Supported," implying that the official government portal cannot accept a corrected application. Thailand's Immigration Bureau, however, allows travelers to update many details using "Update Arrival Card" and submit a new form for information that cannot be edited; the latest valid submission takes precedence. The website also advertises "Unlimited" travelers per submission while describing its simultaneous capacity as 25 to 80 profiles, without explaining how those figures relate. These comparisons may exaggerate the private service's advantages and misrepresent the free government portal's correction options and group-submission capabilities.

## Privacy Concerns

The website processes sensitive passport, contact, and travel information through its private TDAC service. Its privacy policy permits sharing information with affiliates and service providers, and separately allows sharing with unnamed sister companies for marketing purposes. Yet the same policy states that personally identifiable information will not be shared with third parties, leaving the scope of these exceptions unclear. Its separate data protection policy also permits sister-company sharing for promotions. Neither policy specifies a definite retention period for TDAC submissions, referring instead to general legal and operational requirements. Travelers therefore lack clear information about who may receive their records and how long they are retained.

## Billing or Service Issues

The company's TDAC refund policy offers full refunds for canceled trips or services canceled more than seven days before arrival, but does not explain how billing disputes, submission errors, or delays are handled. The policy also describes TDAC submissions as "visa applications," although the TDAC is not a visa.

Public complaints about this TDAC submission service describe payment demands, unexpected charges, refund requests, and unanswered support inquiries. A reviewer on the `tdac.in.th` Trustpilot page reported encountering a payment demand at the final application stage and abandoning the process without paying. Separately, a commenter on `tdac.agents.co.th` reported being charged within the advertised free 72-hour window, including duplicate charges involving two applicants. Another comment requested a refund after payment. A reviewer on the `tdac.agents.co.th` Trustpilot page reported difficulty completing an application because of an address-code issue, with live chat and email inquiries going unanswered for more than six weeks.
