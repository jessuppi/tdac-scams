# Thailand TDAC Scams

This repository documents suspected scams, misleading websites, and deceptive commercial services targeting travelers applying for the Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC).

The TDAC is a mandatory online arrival form introduced by Thailand's Immigration Bureau on May 1, 2025, replacing the traditional paper TM.6 arrival and departure card. All foreign travelers entering Thailand by air, land, or sea are generally required to complete the TDAC online no more than three days (72 hours) before arrival, subject to limited exemptions. **The application is completely free when submitted through the official Thai government website.**

Despite the official application being completely free, numerous third-party websites charge travelers for TDAC services. Some may misrepresent their government affiliation, imply that payment is mandatory, imitate official immigration websites, or conceal the availability of the free government service.

## Official TDAC Website

**The only official Thailand Digital Arrival Card website is:**

https://tdac.immigration.go.th/

TDAC applications submitted through Thailand's Immigration Bureau are completely free. Travelers should avoid third-party TDAC websites, which may collect sensitive passport details and other personal or financial information for fraudulent or otherwise malicious purposes. Providing this information to untrusted services can expose travelers to identity theft, phishing, email spam, financial fraud, and other forms of data misuse.

## Common Warning Signs

Misleading or fraudulent TDAC websites may exhibit the following warning signs:

- **Government impersonation:** Falsely claiming or implying affiliation with Thai immigration authorities.
- **Misleading fees:** Suggesting payment is mandatory for the free TDAC application.
- **Deceptive promotion:** Using misleading advertisements, fake reviews, astroturfing, or black-hat SEO tactics to manipulate public perception, credibility, or search visibility.
- **Hidden disclosures:** Obscuring commercial status, pricing, or the availability of the free official service.
- **Misleading claims:** Providing inaccurate information about requirements, deadlines, processing, or approval.
- **Privacy concerns:** Failing to clearly explain how passport, personal, or financial information is handled.
- **Billing or service issues:** Documented unexpected charges, unauthorized subscriptions, or failure to deliver promised services.

## Documentation Standards

Each website is evaluated using publicly available information, including website content, pricing, advertisements, screenshots, archived pages, and independent sources.

Listings identify specific concerns, provide supporting evidence, and distinguish verified facts from unconfirmed allegations. Information is reviewed for accuracy and updated when new evidence becomes available.

Future domain comparison tables use ✓ for a documented concern, — for no documented concern, and ? for not yet assessed. Each ✓ must have supporting evidence.

Website operators and other interested parties may submit verifiable corrections or clarifications to ensure the documentation remains accurate by opening a GitHub Issue.

## Disclaimer

This repository is an independent public-interest project with no affiliation or endorsement from Thailand's Immigration Bureau or any other government agency.

Inclusion in this repository does not establish criminal wrongdoing, and charging for third-party TDAC assistance alone is not proof of fraud. Findings reflect publicly available evidence and should not be interpreted as official legal or regulatory determinations.
